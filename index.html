What's on the farm

A living farm, not a static screen

🐔
Living Flock

Up to 16 hens roam the yard, lay eggs, and wander toward the highway. Add a rooster, hatch chicks, and discover the legendary golden Clover Hen.

🛣️
Roadside Stand

Cars and vans pull off the highway to buy your eggs. Serve them fast to chain a combo multiplier — the bigger the chain, the bigger the bonus.

🌦️
Dynamic Weather

Rain, wind, and puddles play out in real time across a 120-second day/night cycle. Every session feels different because the farm is always alive.

🦅
Crows & the Farm Dog

Crows swoop in to raid your eggs — but your loyal dog patrols the yard and chases them off. A small drama plays out every day.

📦
Delivery Contracts

Trucks arrive with time-limited orders for eggs, birds, and produce. Fulfil them before the deadline for big rewards and new unlocks.

🍀
Lucky Clovers

Four-leaf clovers sprout randomly across the yard. Tap them before they vanish to collect luck bonuses that boost your earnings for the next run.

A day on the farm

Customers pull up to the stand. Serve them fast to chain combos and stack your coin total.
